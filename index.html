Questions

Before you call

Can you guarantee approval?
No, and nobody can — the decision is the Home Office's. If we think a case will fail, we tell you before you instruct us.
Do I need to travel to the UK?
No. Everything is done by video call and secure upload. You attend only your biometrics appointment in Türkiye.
I was refused before. Can I reapply?
Usually yes, once we have read the refusal notice. Most refusals turn on evidence and can be put right.
Is the Ankara Agreement still open?
Not to new applicants. If you already hold ECAA leave, extensions and settlement remain available.
How long does it take?
It depends on the route, and Home Office timescales change often. We give you the current figure at your consultation.
